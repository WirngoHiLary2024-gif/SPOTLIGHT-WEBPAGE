Speak With Confidence

Spotlight helps people overcome the fear of public speaking through fun speaking missions, real-world practice, and interactive communication challenges.

About Spotlight

Spotlight is a communication training app designed to help users build confidence when speaking in public. Many people struggle with stage fright, nervousness, or anxiety during presentations.Spotlight transforms the learning process into a fun and engaging experience similar to Duolingo.

How It Works

Users complete daily speaking missions and challenges. The app gradually increases difficulty levels to improve confidence step by step.

Users can also invite friends through invitation links and practice together in speaking rooms and communication exercises.

Features

🎯 Daily Missions

Complete interactive speaking challenges every day.

👥 Practice Together

Train with friends using invitation links and live practice.

🏆 Level System

Unlock new speaking levels and improve gradually.

🎤 Real-Life Exercises

Practice interviews, speeches, debates, and presentations.

Our Aim

Our mission is to help people become confident communicators. Spotlight aims to remove fear, build self-esteem, and make public speaking a skill anyone can master.
